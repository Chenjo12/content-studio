# Sydney — sporty, rich lifestyle vibe (XHS reference)

Every city has its own distinct vibe.

You've got the warm, passionate, chill vibe in Madrid.

You've got the calm, peace, sophisticated vibe in Hokkaido

You've got the hustling and bustling vibe in New York.

And in Sydney you've got the sporty and rich vibe.

There's a reason why everybody is up for some sports as soon as the sun rises.

Honestly I think this place is built for a immersive healthy lifestyle.

The weather is perfect for plenty of outdoor sports activities.

Tennis, kayaking under the harbor bridge, or swimming at iceberg by Bondi Beach, surfing, yoga in the park, hiking, or simply just walking and you will accompany with amazing views.

And there are huge grass field everywhere for any type of sports activities.

I want to emphasize a bit about the swimming at the Iceberg.

It's the best swimming experience I've ever had.

I'm just gonna let the photos do the talk.

The ticket is only 10 AUD, and you get the pool with crazy waves, a proper gym with the best view possible for a workout and a sauna.

What a package for just 10 AUD

It was freezing cold but I loved every bit of it. The amount of dopamine hit is undescribable.

So you've the view, the weather, the nature, the facilities, for a superb exercise. And you need to be rewarded.

Now Sydney offers you their cafe brunch culture to recharge you with the fluffy Ricotta Hotcakes, the smoothest eggs ever, some meaty bacon, and a peanut butter protein shake. OH and of course a solid cup of coffee.

Boom! That's a powerful combo, powerful exercise + super healthy delicious food, no wonder people look so good during summer times in Bondi Beach.

If you want to live healthy and happy Sydney is the place to be in my opinion.
