1. **"The Hotel Where the Staff Didn't Run" — the 2008 Mumbai (26/11) terror attack on the Taj Mahal Palace Hotel, told through the hospitality-training mechanism behind why employees stayed.** On November 26–29, 2008, ten Lashkar-e-Taiba gunmen carried out coordinated attacks across Mumbai; the Taj Mahal Palace Hotel was under siege for roughly 60 hours, with the hotel's iconic dome burning on live television and approximately 31 people killed inside before National Security Guard commandos secured the building on the morning of November 29 ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/event/Mumbai-terrorist-attacks-of-2008), [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2013/09/18/world/asia/mumbai-terror-attacks), [Wikipedia — 2008 Mumbai attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Mumbai_attacks)).
