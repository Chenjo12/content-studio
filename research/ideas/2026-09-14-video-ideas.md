Weeks later, the first **JW Marriott all-inclusive** — a conversion of the former Dreams Las Mareas site into the 415-room JW Marriott Costa Elena in Guanacaste, Costa Rica, including a "Griffin Club" hotel-within-a-hotel — was set to open this same week, now pushed to September 30, 2026 ([Upgraded Points hotel-news roundup](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/hotel-news-roundup-september-week-2-2026/)). Both are Marriott-portfolio brands, both are the brand's literal first all-inclusive property ever, both landing within about six weeks of each other. Running that experiment simultaneously on two different upscale/lifestyle brands in the same portfolio, in the same season, isn't a coincidence — it's a company testing which kind of brand identity survives the all-inclusive format without diluting it, and doing it quietly enough that nobody's naming it as the coordinated test it actually is. Register: comedic-dry insider. Hook: two hotel brands that have spent decades telling you they're too sophisticated for an all-inclusive bracelet just quietly put one on, at the same time, on purpose, and are hoping you don't notice it's the same experiment.
