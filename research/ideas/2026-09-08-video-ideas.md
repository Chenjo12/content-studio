Register: comedic-dry insider. The mechanism worth explaining: revenue-management software is standard, legitimate hotel-operations tooling this creator's training actually covers — the legal question dividing "smart pricing" from "algorithmic collusion" is exactly the kind of insider distinction a generalist travel account can't make correctly. Source: today's pulse-check. Platform(s): YouTube (video essay — the two parallel legal threads need room), Bilibili (knowledge-format/business-mechanism fit). Asset fit: moderate — no direct named-brand hit (only an indirect corporate-parent link to W, which needs careful, accurate framing, not implication), but the mechanism is squarely within hospitality revenue-management literacy. Register: comedic-dry insider.
