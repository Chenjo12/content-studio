2. **"The Hotel Lobby That Became an Operating Theatre."** A genuine calendar coincidence: **today, October 12, 2026, is exactly 114 years since the Palace Hotel Madrid opened its doors on October 12, 1912** ([Hosteltur](https://www.hosteltur.com/124577_the-westin-palace-madrid-celebra-sus-105-anos-precios-1912.html), confirming the 1912 opening date). During the Spanish Civil War (1936-1939), the hotel — closed only this one other time in its history — was requisitioned and converted into a military hospital; its signature glass-domed lounge, built to flood the Belle Époque lobby with natural light for elegance, was repurposed as an operating theatre precisely because that light made it the best-lit room in the building ([Olive Press](https://theolivepress.es/spain-news/2021/01/19/royal-treatment-madrids-westin-palace-hotel-is-used-to-hosting-the-rich-and-famous-from-michael-jackson-to-ernest-hemingway/), [Galerie Magazine](https://galeriemagazine.com/hotel-of-the-week-the-palace-madrid/)). After the war, the hotel was restored in 1939 at a cost of four million pesetas and later became a magnet for figures like Hemingway, Orson Welles, and Picasso in its post-war decades ([Olive Press](https://theolivepress.es/spain-news/2021/01/19/royal-treatment-madrids-westin-palace-hotel-is-used-to-hosting-the-rich-and-famous-from-michael-jackson-to-ernest-hemingway/)). **Honest limitation, stated rather than papered over:** sources disagree on Hemingway's specific Civil War-era Madrid base — several place his 1937 wartime correspondent work at the Hotel Florida (since demolished) or Gran Vía 25, not the Palace — so a script must keep the Palace's own Civil War claim (hospital/operating-theatre use) separate from general "Hemingway in Madrid" lore rather than conflating the two; `story-researcher` should verify the operating-theatre claim against additional primary sources before scripting, per CLAUDE.md's extra-rigor bar for real historic events. Confirmed genuinely new via grep for "Palace Hotel Madrid" and "Westin Palace" across all 63 prior batches and the production queue — no hits before today.
