Marriott is piloting refillable dispensers and bar-soap-without-plastic as the replacement. Same "total sincerity on something tiny" mechanism as "The Rise of the Minibar," but this one gets a real ending: legislation is killing it on-camera-timeline. — Source: today's pulse-check ([The Cool Down](https://www.thecooldown.com/green-business/ban-single-use-plastic-bottles-hotels-illinois/), [Daily Passport](https://dailypassport.com/tiny-shampoo-hotels-disappearing-states-ban-single-use-plastics/), [Packaging Dive](https://www.packagingdive.com/news/hotel-marriott-plastic-washington-law-refill/651512/)). Platform(s): YouTube. Asset fit: strong — hospitality-ops knowledge explains why hotels resisted this change for so long (guest-perceived luxury vs. compliance cost), which a generic sustainability-news treatment won't cover. Register: comedic. Hook: an object about to legally cease to exist, treated with the gravity of a corporate obituary.
