2. **"The Room Across the Hall From Yours Right Now Is a Different Price — and It Has Nothing to Do With Loyalty Status."** A straight insider explainer on hotel revenue management: the software (an RMS layered on the property's booking system) that repriced your room in real time based on booking pace, competitor rates, and how many rooms are left — the same discipline taught as its own course in hospitality-management programs, not a dark-pattern conspiracy. Industry benchmarks put the revenue lift from dynamic pricing at 10-25% over flat rates, and AI-driven pricing/personalization is one of the hospitality sector's most-cited 2026 trends. The hook is demystifying, not exposing: most guests assume price differences mean something about *them* (status, negotiation, luck) when it's almost always about *timing* relative to a demand curve nobody outside the industry sees. — Source: experimental, grounded in today's pulse-check on 2026 hospitality-AI trend coverage ([NetSuite — dynamic pricing mechanics](https://www.netsuite.com/portal/resource/articles/business-strategy/hotel-dynamic-pricing.shtml), [Kody Technolab — how it works behind the scenes](https://kodytechnolab.com/blog/how-hotel-dynamic-pricing-works/)). Platform(s): YouTube, Bilibili (knowledge-product potential — this is dense enough to seed a short paid-column explainer per CLAUDE.md's Bilibili note). Asset fit: strong — this is literally Swiss hotel-management-school curriculum, the kind of mechanism knowledge a generalist travel channel can't produce. Register: comedic-dry insider. Hook: "the number on your booking confirmation was never really about you."

Platform(s): YouTube, XHS. Asset fit: strong — real hospitality-industry judgment about inventory economics, not just a funny screenshot reaction. Register: comedic.
