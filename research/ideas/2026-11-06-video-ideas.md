The actual industrial process is the funny, specific part a generalist travel account would never dig into: hotel housekeeping collects discarded bars into bins, Clean the World picks them up, then **shreds, sanitizes, re-melts, and repackages them into new bars** distributed to disaster-relief zones, refugee camps, and developing communities — your hotel soap, in its literal physical form, might end up as someone else's soap bar on the other side of the world. **Real scale, with named luxury-hotel partners**: Clean the World now works with over **8,600 hospitality partners**, having diverted more than **31 million pounds of waste from landfills** and distributed roughly **80–87 million bars of soap across ~127 countries** since 2009 (figures climb year over year across sources — 40M by early reporting, 80M in 2023, 87M in the organization's most recent blog post — treat the trajectory as real, the exact current number as approximate).
