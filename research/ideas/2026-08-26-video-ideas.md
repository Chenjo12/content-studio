## Ideas

1. **"The Hotel That Got Built Because No Hotel Would Let a Woman Check In Alone."** Hotel Figueroa in downtown LA turns 100 on August 28, 2026 (two days from now) — genuinely fresh, with an actual centennial event on that date, not a round-number-anniversary reach. The real story: in the 1920s, unaccompanied women were routinely refused rooms at American hotels without a male chaperone, treated as a reputational risk to the property. The LA YWCA, led by Mrs. Chester C. Ashley, raised the money itself — two mortgage bonds plus supporter funding — and built a 409-room hotel at 939 S. Figueroa St. specifically so solo female travelers had somewhere to stay, opening August 14, 1926. It went on to install Maude Bouldin as the first female hotel managing director in the US. The insider read this creator is positioned to give that a generic history channel isn't: this wasn't a symbolic gesture, it was a genuine hospitality-operations problem (the industry's own gatekeeping mechanics — front-desk discretion, "reputation of the house," who a hotel decides is an acceptable guest) solved by building an entirely separate institution rather than petitioning existing ones to change. And it's a throughline worth pulling into the present tense: hospitality is majority-female at the front line and still disproportionately male at ownership/GM level even today — Figueroa's "first female managing director" framing isn't just a period detail, it's a comparison point that still bites. — Source: today's pulse-check ([FOX 11 LA](https://www.foxla.com/news/hotel-figueroa-continuing-history-of-celebrating-women), [PBS SoCal](https://www.pbssocal.org/history-society/the-figueroa-hotel-the-surprising-feminist-origins-of-a-sexy-downtown-staple), [LAist](https://laist.com/news/entertainment/how-the-hotel-figueroa-went-from-ywca-haven-to-hipster-hangout), [Capital Hall Partners](https://www.caphall.com/where-women-made-history-the-hotel-figueroa/), [Patch — centennial event listing, Aug 28](https://patch.com/california/los-angeles/calendar/event/20260828/80ae8460-107d-4d0a-9a3b-b9c553381d1e/hotel-figueroa-s-100-year-anniversary-celebration)).
