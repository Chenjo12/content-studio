2. **"The Hotel Built By Women, For Women, Because You Legally Couldn't Check In Without a Man."** Hotel Figueroa in downtown Los Angeles turns 100 on August 28, 2026 (six days out) — the YWCA opened it in 1926 as the largest commercial building funded by women, for women, at a time when most hotels wouldn't let a woman check in without a male chaperone. It was led by the country's first female hotel managing director, Maude Bouldin, and was — per the hotel's own account and multiple retrospectives — the only project of its scale where women ran and designed every inch of it; the hotel didn't open its floors to men until February 1928, nearly two years later. It became a real hub for traveling professional women, with its coffee shop and salons functioning as an informal collective for art and activism. This is hotel-history-as-deep-dive per CLAUDE.md's own category, but distinct in register from every prior history entry used so far (Ritz Paris under occupation, the Manila Hotel, the Manor Hotel and Hyatt Regency KC disasters) — those are all tragedy/disaster stories; this is real institutional history with genuine stakes (legal and social exclusion of women from hotels) but a story that ends in something built and won, not lost — worth naming as a different flavor of "gripping" so scriptwriter doesn't default to a somber register that doesn't fit the material. No direct creator collaboration-brand tie (the property is now Hyatt's Unbound Collection) — flagged honestly as an editorial-coherence stretch, but a strong one: Swiss hotel-management training gives real, non-obvious things to say here (what it actually took operationally to run a hotel entirely staffed and managed by women in 1926, not just the headline fact of it). — Source: today's pulse-check ([Forbes — Hotel Figueroa centennial](https://www.forbes.com/sites/michaelgoldstein/2026/02/24/the-hotel-figueroa-celebrates-a-century-in-downtown-los-angeles/), [LAist — YWCA haven to hipster hangout](https://laist.com/news/entertainment/how-the-hotel-figueroa-went-from-ywca-haven-to-hipster-hangout), [FOX 11 LA](https://www.foxla.com/news/hotel-figueroa-continuing-history-of-celebrating-women), [Hotel Figueroa's own history page](https://www.hotelfigueroa.com/our-hotel/history/)). Platform(s): YouTube (video-essay format). Asset fit: moderate — no brand tie, but a strong fit for the hospitality-history category and the video-essay strength CLAUDE.md names directly. Register: history — gripping register (institutional/social-history variant, not disaster).
