# Four Seasons Resort Bali at Sayan

## Facts

- **Architect & concept:** Designed by London-based architect John Heah (who cites Carlo Scarpa and Frank Lloyd Wright as influences). Heah's own framing: "You can imagine the Resort literally as a bowl of rice. I wanted people to arrive in the middle of that space." [[Pursuitist](https://pursuitist.com/new-architecture-journey-at-four-seasons-sayan-bali/)]
- **The "rice bowl" building:** The resort's signature element is an elliptical, upside-down main building — variously described as a "UFO-shaped" structure or "giant rice bowl" — that guests descend through, from a rooftop lotus pond down through curved architecture, before the valley view reveals itself. It houses the lobby, restaurant, health club, spa, and part of the suite inventory. [[Pursuitist](https://pursuitist.com/new-architecture-journey-at-four-seasons-sayan-bali/)] [[GDC Interiors](https://www.gdcinteriors.com/four-seasons-bali-sayan/)]
- **Arrival experience:** Guests arrive via a wooden footbridge suspended above the Sayan Valley, crossing over a UNESCO-listed *subak* (traditional Balinese sustainable irrigation) system on the river below — a genuinely distinctive detail most amenity-focused coverage doesn't dig into. [[Pursuitist](https://pursuitist.com/new-architecture-journey-at-four-seasons-sayan-bali/)]
- **Scale:** 18 suites within the main building plus 42 private villas cascading down the hillside toward the Ayung River — 60 total keys. [[Web search summary, corroborated across multiple listings]]
- **Grounds:** On-site rice fields, fruit tree groves, and vegetable/herb gardens integrated into the property rather than purely ornamental landscaping. [[Pursuitist](https://pursuitist.com/new-architecture-journey-at-four-seasons-sayan-bali/)]
- **Just-launched 2026 refresh (timely, editorially useful hook):** A sustainability-led renovation of 7 suites (5 Premier Duplex Suites, 2 Family Premier Suites) plus both dining venues (Ayung Terrace restaurant, Jati Bar), led by Hirsch Bedner Associates (HBA), launching **July 2026** — i.e. this just happened. Framed internally as "refined, rejuvenated, and liveable," anchored in "Balinese harmony and heritage." Concrete sustainability measures: energy-efficient LED lighting, refrigerant-friendly A/C units, reuse/refurbishment of existing materials over new sourcing, locally-sourced durable materials. [[Four Seasons Press](https://press.fourseasons.com/sayan/hotel-news/2026/sustainability-led-refresh/)]
